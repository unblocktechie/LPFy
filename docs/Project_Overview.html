LPFY

How LPFY Markets work

Isolated per-pair ERC-4626 vaults hold idle USDC. Borrowers post Uniswap LP NFTs and borrow under an LTV cap. Liquidations repay the vault, then split surplus between feeTo and the borrower. Pricing uses Chainlink; liquidations can run via CRE.

1. Overview

Lenders

ERC-4626 PairVault

One vault per pair. Supply with deposit / redeem. Idle USDC sits in the vault, not on the market.

Borrowers

LP NFT collateral

Lock a Uniswap V3/V4 LP NFT and borrow Circle USDC from the matching pair under LTV limits.

Fees

Surplus on liquidate

liquidationFeeBps of post-debt surplus → feeTo (separate from Ownable owner). Rest → borrower.

Lenders supply through PairVault.deposit. The market does not take direct lender deposits — it only pulls and pushes vault liquidity for borrows and repayments.

4. Share pricing

Share price = totalAssets / totalSupply. PairVault overrides totalAssets so lenders accrue when borrowers owe interest — not only when cash sits idle.

totalAssets() = idleAssets() + assets owed by the market
             = USDC idle in vault
             + totalPrincipal + totalAccruedInterest
Accrued interest raises share value. Converting that value to USDC still needs cash returned via repay or liquidation.

5. Liquidation fee structure

Fees apply only on successful liquidation when recovery exceeds debt. They are configured on the market, not on CRE or the vault.

Variable Meaning
liquidationFeeBps % of surplus (after vault debt repaid), in basis points. 1000 = 10%
feeTo Wallet that receives the protocol fee — independent of Ownable owner
Borrower surplus Remaining surplus after the fee
Shortfall If recovery < debt → no fee, no surplus to borrower
Admin (owner only): setLiquidationFeeBps · setFeeTo

Surplus split

debt repaid to PairVault = principal + interest
surplus = recoveryUSDC − debt
fee = surplus × liquidationFeeBps / 10000 → feeTo
borrower gets = surplus − fee

6. Liquidation path

  1. Loan is underwater (debt / collateral ≥ liquidation threshold).
  2. Owner or CRE liquidation receiver calls liquidate.
  3. Adapter unwinds LP NFT → collect tokens (empty NFT → borrower).
  4. Non-USDC legs are swapped to Circle USDC.
  5. Debt is pushed back into the pair’s PairVault.
  6. liquidationFeeBps of remaining surplus → feeTo.
  7. Leftover surplus → borrower; loan closed.

7. Who does what

Lender

Deposits and redeems USDC via the pair vault. Earns from borrower interest through share price.

Borrower

Posts LP NFT collateral, borrows USDC, repays debt, and withdraws the NFT when debt is zero.

Owner

Configures risk params, fees, and may liquidate. Liquidate and admin actions are owner-only in the app.

CRE

Automated keeper that detects underwater loans and triggers liquidate. Does not set fees.

feeTo

Receives the protocol cut of liquidation surplus when recovery exceeds debt.

Oracles

Chainlink Data Feeds price collateral for LTV and liquidation health checks.

Quick recap

  • Isolated pools: each pair has its own books and PairVault.
  • ERC-4626: lenders use standard deposit / mint / withdraw / redeem.
  • Custody: idle USDC in vault; market pulls on borrow, pushes on repay / liquidate.
  • Pricing: totalAssets = idle + principal + accrued interest.
  • Fees: after vault is repaid, liquidationFeeBps of surplus → feeTo; rest → borrower.
  • Authority: liquidate is owner or CRE only.
