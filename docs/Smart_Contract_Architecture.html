1. Overview

Lenders supply Circle USDC into isolated per-pair ERC-4626 PairVaults. Borrowers lock a DEX V3 or V4 LP NFT and borrow USDC from the matching pair. Interest accrues on-chain. Underwater loans are liquidated by the market owner or Chainlink CRE via CreLiquidationReceiver. After debt is pushed back to the vault, a protocol fee may go to feeTo.

Idle USDC lives in PairVault, not on the market. Legacy lendUsdc / withdrawLender revert (UsePairVault).
Canonical address file: smart-contracts/deployments/sepolia-markets.json. Repo: github.com/unblocktechie/LPFy.

sequenceDiagram
  actor Auth as Owner / CRE
  participant Market as MarketLendingModule
  participant Adapter as Adapter

  participant Vault as PairVault
  participant FeeTo as feeTo

6. Fee structure (on market)

Knob Meaning
liquidationFeeBps % of surplus after vault debt repaid (1000 = 10%)
feeTo Fee recipient — independent of Ownable owner
Order on liquidate: repay PairVault → fee to feeTo → remainder to borrower. Shortfall → no fee. Set via setLiquidationFeeBps / setFeeTo or redeploy env vars.
